Frequently asked questions

From first ideas to final details, here’s how working with Shmel looks. Select a question to read the answer.

How much does it cost to work with Shmel Agency?

Every project is different. The cost depends on your goals, the services you need, the scope of the project and the level of support required.

You can work with us on an individual service or combine several services into a more comprehensive solution. We’ll discuss your priorities, explore the options that make sense for your business and agree on a scope that fits your needs and budget.

Not sure what you need yet? That’s absolutely fine — we can figure it out together.

Can we work together if I have a limited budget?

Yes! We can adapt the project to your budget by focusing on the services and tasks that will make the biggest difference for your business. Whether you need one specific service or a combination of several, we’ll work with you to find the right approach and create a solution that fits your goals and budget.

If you’re not sure where to start, we can discuss your needs together and recommend the most suitable options.

What services does Shmel Agency offer?

Shmel Agency combines branding, marketing strategy and creative execution to help businesses build, communicate and grow their brands.

Our services include brand strategy and visual identity, marketing strategy and consulting, audience research, content strategy, social media design and planning, website and digital design, packaging and print design, and AI Search Readiness and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

You can choose individual services or combine them into a tailored project based on your business needs.

Do I need to know exactly which services I need before contacting you?

Not at all. You can come to us with a clear brief, a rough idea or simply a problem you want to solve.

We will learn about your business, discuss your goals and identify which services or combination of services could be most useful. Our role is not just to deliver what you ask for, but to help you understand what your business actually needs.

Does Shmel Agency work with startups and small businesses?

Yes. We work with startups, new businesses, cafés and restaurants, creators, personal brands, small and growing businesses, and projects that need a fresh perspective.

Whether you are launching a new idea, improving an existing brand or trying to reach a new audience, we tailor the scope of work to your goals and stage of development.

Can you help if my business idea is still at an early stage?

Yes. You do not need to have a finished brand, a complete strategy or a perfectly defined business plan.

We can help you explore your positioning, understand your audience, develop your brand concept and decide which steps to prioritise before launch. The exact scope depends on how developed your idea is and what support you need.

What is the process of working with Shmel Agency?

Our process is built around collaboration and clear expectations.

1. Tell us about your project. Share your idea, goals and any relevant materials.

2. Let’s talk. We discuss your needs, challenges and expectations in an online or in-person meeting.

3. Receive a proposal. We develop a suitable direction, scope, timeline and budget options.

4. Finalise the plan. We agree on deliverables, responsibilities and terms before starting.

5. Bring the project to life. We work together, share progress and refine the agreed deliverables through feedback.

Every project is different, so the exact process may vary depending on the services involved.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. We offer a free initial discussion to learn about your business, understand your goals and explore whether we are a good fit for your project.

After receiving your enquiry, we aim to get back to you within 2–5 business days. The conversation helps us understand your needs before recommending a scope of work.

How many revisions are included in a project?

Unless otherwise stated in your agreement, each deliverable includes two rounds of revisions.

A revision round means one consolidated set of feedback submitted at the same time. Additional revisions, major changes in direction or requests outside the agreed scope may require an additional fee. All deliverables and revision terms are confirmed before production begins.

Can Shmel Agency help my business appear in AI search results?

We offer AI Search Readiness and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) to help businesses make their online information easier for search engines and AI-powered discovery tools to find, understand and reference.

Depending on your needs, this may include improving website structure and content, strengthening technical SEO foundations, implementing relevant structured data and creating clear, useful answers to customer questions.

AI search visibility depends on many factors, and no agency can guarantee that a particular AI tool will recommend or cite your business. Our focus is on building a stronger, more accessible and more informative digital presence.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps your website become easier to discover and understand through traditional search engines such as Google and Bing.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on improving how your business and its content may be discovered, understood and referenced in AI-generated answers.

The two overlap. Clear content, accessible pages, trustworthy information, relevant external references and strong technical foundations can support both traditional search visibility and AI discovery. GEO does not guarantee recommendations from AI tools.
