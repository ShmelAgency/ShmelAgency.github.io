How do we work?

A little conversation. A clear direction. A lot of buzz.
Open a step to see what happens next.

Step 0Tell Us About You↓

Start by sending us a message and letting us know about your company, your project, and what you’re looking for. Share any relevant information, ideas, links, or materials that might help us understand your vision.

We’ll review everything and get back to you within 2–5 business days.

Step 1Let’s Talk↓

Once we’ve learned a little more about you and your project, we’ll arrange either an online or in-person meeting. This is our chance to get to know each other properly, talk through your goals, expectations, challenges, and ideas, and figure out exactly what you need from us.

Our Suggestion: Grab a Tea, Coffee, or whatever you’d like and get comfortable. We want this to feel like less of a formal business meeting and more like a good conversation about the ideas you’re excited to bring to life.

Step 2Behind-The-Scenes↓

Now it’s our turn to get to work.

We’ll take everything we discussed, research your company and market where necessary, organize our ideas, and develop a clear direction for the project.

Depending on what you need, this may include a presentation, creative concepts, marketing strategies, content plans, visual direction, campaign proposals, or other tailored solutions.

Your job during this part is to just relax and let us work our magic.

Step 3Finalising the plan↓

Once the initial direction is ready, we’ll meet again to walk you through our ideas, answer your questions, and make sure everything feels just right.

Together, we’ll finalise the scope of work, services, timeline, deliverables, and any other important details. Once everything is agreed upon, we’ll sign the agreement and officially get started.

Step 4Production↓

This is where the project comes to life.

Throughout the production process, we’ll stay in close contact with you, share progress when appropriate, and make sure you always know where the project stands.

Your feedback is an important part of the process, so each deliverable includes two rounds of revisions unless otherwise stated in your agreement.

A revision round means one collected set of feedback submitted at the same time. Additional revisions, major changes in direction, or requests outside the agreed project scope may require an additional fee.

The goal is simple: clear communication, no surprises, and work we’re both happy to put our names on.

What do we SERVE?

A few questions before we start?

How much does it cost to work with Shmel Agency?

Every project is different. The cost depends on your goals, the services you need, the scope of the project and the level of support required.

You can work with us on an individual service or combine several services into a more comprehensive solution. We’ll discuss your priorities, explore the options that make sense for your business and agree on a scope that fits your needs and budget.

Not sure what you need yet? That’s absolutely fine — we can figure it out together.

Do I need to know exactly which services I need before contacting you?

Not at all. You can come to us with a clear brief, a rough idea or simply a problem you want to solve.

We will learn about your business, discuss your goals and identify which services or combination of services could be most useful. Our role is not just to deliver what you ask for, but to help you understand what your business actually needs.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. We offer a free initial discussion to learn about your business, understand your goals and explore whether we are a good fit for your project.

After receiving your enquiry, we aim to get back to you within 2–5 business days. The conversation helps us understand your needs before recommending a scope of work.
