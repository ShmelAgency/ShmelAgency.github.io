The services enquiry form

The form creates a PDF directly in your browser. Your answers are not submitted to Shmel when you select services or download the PDF. The website does not save your answers to browser storage. Closing or reloading the page clears the form; your downloaded PDF remains on your device until you delete it.

Email enquiries

Email links open your email application. You decide whether to send a message and whether to attach your PDF. Sending an email shares its contents and attachments with Shmel through your email provider and our mailbox provider. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us.

Cookies and browser storage

The website code does not set cookies or include analytics, advertising pixels. The Video and Photo gallery plays video files served by this website. It uses a session-storage entry, shmel-mood, solely to remember the mood you select as you move between pages in the same tab. This contains only a theme code, not personal or form information. Closing the tab ends these preferences; your browser’s site-data settings can also clear them.

Hosting and external services

Loading a hosted website sends connection information, such as your IP address and requested files, to the hosting service. Hosting logs and any hosting-level cookies depend on the service used to publish this website. Email handling also depends on the mailbox service. Fonts, artwork and styles are served with this website; they are not loaded from a third-party font or stylesheet service.

Questions

For questions about this website or information you have shared with Shmel, email shmelagency.eu@gmail.com. You can also contact the Danish Data Protection Agency about data protection concerns.
